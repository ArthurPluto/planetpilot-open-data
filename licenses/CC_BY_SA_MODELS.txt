Two 3D models in Planet Pilot under CC BY-SA 3.0
================================================

The 3D models of Christ the Redeemer (assets/models/landmarks/christ_redeemer.glb
inside the game) and of Mount Rushmore (assets/models/landmarks/mount_rushmore.glb)
are adaptations of two photographs from Wikimedia Commons. They were made
with TRELLIS.2 (Microsoft, MIT licence) from cropped copies of the photos and
then modified (resized to real size, decimated, recoloured, given simple
bases) by the developer.

1. "Christ the Redeemer - Cristo Redentor" by Arne Mueseler (Arne Müseler)
   https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Christ_the_Redeemer_-_Cristo_Redentor.jpg
   Licence: CC BY-SA 3.0 DE, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/de/deed.en

2. "Mount Rushmore detail view (100MP)" by Thomas Wolf, www.foto-tw.de
   https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mount_Rushmore_detail_view_(100MP).jpg
   Licence: CC BY-SA 3.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/
